Frequently Asked Questions

My progress was lost after reinstalling the app.

Game progress (high score, coins, upgrades) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data permanently. There is currently no cloud backup — this is a known limitation we may address in a future update.

The game has no sound.

On iOS, audio requires a screen tap to unlock due to browser restrictions. Tap anywhere on the main menu to activate sound. Also check that your device is not in Silent Mode (ring/silent switch on the side of iPhone).

The game runs slowly or drops frames.

Trigonx targets 60 FPS on most modern devices. If you experience performance issues, try closing background apps and restarting the game. Older devices may run at reduced frame rates.

Haptic feedback is not working.

Make sure haptics are enabled in the in-game settings. Haptic feedback requires a real device and does not work in simulators or emulators.

How do I reset my progress?

Uninstalling and reinstalling the app will clear all saved data. There is no in-game reset option at this time.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Please report it on GitHub Issues. Include your device model and OS version to help us reproduce the issue.
