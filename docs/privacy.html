Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated: April 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Trigonx ("the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to the Trigonx mobile app available on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Trigonx stores game progress data (high score, coins, upgrades, achievements, settings) exclusively on your device using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

2. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device:

  • High score and best level
  • Coins and prestige upgrades
  • Achievement progress
  • Audio and haptic settings
  • Daily challenge state

This data is managed entirely by your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

3. Third-Party Services

Trigonx does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or social login providers. No data is shared with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Trigonx is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no data is collected at all, there is no risk of collecting children's data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Support page.
